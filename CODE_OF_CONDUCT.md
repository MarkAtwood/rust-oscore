# Code of Conduct

This project exists to produce working software. Conduct rules exist
only to protect that.

## Scope

This document applies to this project's repositories, issue tracker,
and other channels the project runs. It does not cover anything you
do anywhere else. Conduct outside the project is not reported here,
not investigated here, and not grounds for action here.

## What's judged

Contributions are judged on correctness, quality, and fit with the
project. Nothing about the contributor beyond their work is relevant
or considered.

## What's not allowed

- Disrupting the work: spam, flooding, deliberate derailing, brigading
- Threats, and publishing others' private information
- Sustained personal harassment of a specific participant after being told to stop

Blunt technical criticism is not on this list. "This is wrong, here's why"
is the job. Being offended is not, by itself, a violation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers decide. Typical responses are locking a thread, closing an
issue, or blocking an account. There are no committees, no investigations,
and no public statements. If you disagree with a maintainer's decision,
you are free to fork. That is what the license is for.
